## ⭐ **Daily Use (30 Seconds)**

### Morning: Start Recording

```
1. Open bookmark
   ↓
2. Your device ID already there! ✅
   ↓
3. Click "Start recording"
   ↓
4. Share screen:
   - Choose "Chrome Tab" or "Entire Screen"
   - ✅ Check "Share tab audio" (IMPORTANT for call audio!)
   - Click "Share"
   ↓
5. Done! ✅ Recording streams automatically
```

### During Day

**Check occasionally:**
- Status shows: "Recording & streaming..."
- "Chunks uploaded" number increases every 5 seconds
- Both = everything working! ✅

**Keep browser tab open** (minimized is OK, just don't close it)

### Evening: Stop Recording

```
1. Click "Stop recording"
   ↓
2. Wait for "All chunks uploaded"
   ↓
3. Close browser
   ↓
4. Done! ✅
```

## ❓ **Common Questions**

### Q: Do I type my device ID every day?

**A: NO!** ✅ 

Only first time. Browser remembers forever.

---

### Q: What if I forget my device ID?

**A:** Ask admin. Device IDs are:
- teacher-01
- teacher-02
- teacher-03
- ... (etc.)

---

### Q: Can I change my device ID?

**A:** Yes! Just type new ID and browser saves it.

---

### Q: What if I close the tab by accident?

**A:** Current recording stops (that segment is lost). 

Just open again and start new recording. Your ID is still saved!

---

### Q: Can I minimize the browser?

**A:** Yes! ✅ Minimizing is fine. Just don't close the tab.

---

### Q: I don't hear my call audio in recording!

**A:** You forgot to enable "Share tab audio"!

**Fix:**
1. Stop recording
2. Start again
3. This time: ✅ **Check "Share tab audio"** ← Important!
4. Click "Share"

---

### Q: Where do recordings go?

**A:** Admin laptop automatically saves them.

You don't need to do anything! Just record and stop. ✅

---

### Q: How do I know it's working?

**A:** Watch these:

✅ Status: "Recording & streaming..."  
✅ "Chunks uploaded" increases every 5 seconds  
✅ "Upload queue" shows "0 chunks queued"  

All good = working!

---

### Q: What if internet disconnects?

**A:** Chunks will queue in browser memory.

When internet returns, they upload automatically.

**But:** If you close tab before queue clears, those chunks are lost.

---

### Q: My laptop/Chromebook goes to sleep. Problem?

**A:** YES! Recording stops when device sleeps.

**Solution:** Keep laptop lid open during recording.

---

### Q: Do I need to delete anything from my Chromebook?

**A: NO!** ✅

This system uses **zero Chromebook storage**.

Nothing saves to your Downloads folder. Nothing to delete!

---

## 🚨 **Troubleshooting**

### Problem: Can't open the URL

**Solutions:**
1. Check you're connected to academy Wi-Fi
2. Ask admin for correct IP address
3. Try refreshing page (Ctrl+Shift+R)

---

### Problem: "Chunks uploaded" not increasing

**Meaning:** Upload not working

**Solutions:**
1. Check internet connection
2. Ask admin if server is running
3. Restart recording

---

### Problem: "Upload queue" keeps growing

**Meaning:** Uploads failing or slow

**Solutions:**
1. Wait a bit (might be temporary)
2. Check internet connection
3. If very large queue (100+), restart recording

---

### Problem: No audio in recording

**Cause:** Forgot "Share tab audio"

**Fix:**
1. Stop recording
2. Start again
3. ✅ Check "Share tab audio" this time!
