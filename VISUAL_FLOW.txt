┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ COMMON MISTAKES & FIXES                                              │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

❌ MISTAKE: Forgot to check "Share tab audio"
   RESULT: Only your voice recorded, friend silent
   FIX: Stop and restart, remember to check the box!

❌ MISTAKE: Selected "Entire screen" instead of "Chrome Tab"
   RESULT: Works but "Share tab audio" option missing
   FIX: Select "Chrome Tab" specifically

❌ MISTAKE: Started recording before WhatsApp call started
   RESULT: No call audio to share
   FIX: Start WhatsApp call FIRST, then start recording

❌ MISTAKE: Closed WhatsApp Web tab during recording
   RESULT: Recording stops immediately
   FIX: Keep ALL tabs open until done

❌ MISTAKE: Closed recorder tab during call
   RESULT: Recording stops, uploads lost
   FIX: Keep recorder tab open (can minimize browser)
